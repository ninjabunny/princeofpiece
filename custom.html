~ Custom Orders ~

"If you can imagine it, we can craft it."

At Prince of Piece, we believe the finest puzzles are those made for a specific person and purpose. Our bespoke commission service lets you collaborate directly with our master craftsmen to create a one-of-a-kind mechanical puzzle.

-=*=- -=*=- -=*=- -=*=- -=*=-

Our Process

Step Description Timeline
1 Consultation
We discuss your vision, requirements, and budget. What should the puzzle do? Who is it for? What's the occasion?		 1-2 days
2 Design
Our designers create detailed plans and mechanism sketches. You review and approve before we begin.		 1-2 weeks
3 Prototype
We build a working prototype and test the mechanism thoroughly. Adjustments are made as needed.		 2-3 weeks
4 Final Build
The finished puzzle is crafted from your chosen materials with full finishing and quality checks.		 1-2 weeks
5 Delivery
Your puzzle is shipped in a custom presentation box with a certificate of authenticity.		 3-5 days

Commission Types

Engagement & Proposal Boxes

Our most popular custom order! A puzzle box that holds an engagement ring. Your partner must solve the puzzle to discover the ring inside. We can incorporate personal symbols, dates, initials, or inside jokes into the mechanism.

Starting at $350

Corporate Gifts & Awards

Custom puzzles make unforgettable corporate gifts. We can incorporate your company logo, branding colours, and messaging into unique puzzle designs. Bulk orders of 10+ receive a discount. Perfect for executive gifts, team-building events, or awards.

Starting at $150/unit (bulk discounts available)

Competition & Exhibition Puzzles

Need a puzzle for a competition, exhibition, or museum display? We build to competition specifications and can create puzzles at any difficulty level. Previous clients include the International Puzzle Party and the Smithsonian.

Starting at $500

Personal Commissions

Birthday gifts, anniversaries, retirement presents, or just because. Tell us about the person and the occasion, and we'll design a puzzle that's perfect for them. Every commission is completely unique.

Starting at $200

Pricing Guide

Custom puzzle pricing depends on complexity, materials, and size. Here's a general guide:

Complexity Description Price Range

Moderate Multi-step mechanism, mixed materials, medium size $250 - $450
Complex Advanced mechanism, premium materials, larger size $450 - $800
Masterwork Museum-quality, exotic materials, extensive mechanism $800+
