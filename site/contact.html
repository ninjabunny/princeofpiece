Quick FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Do you ship internationally?
A: Yes! We ship worldwide. International shipping rates vary by destination. Email us for a quote.

Q: How long does shipping take?
A: Domestic orders ship within 3-5 business days. International orders take 2-4 weeks depending on destination and customs.

Q: Can I visit the workshop?
A: Absolutely! We love showing visitors around. Please call or email to schedule an appointment.

Q: Do you offer gift wrapping?
A: Every puzzle ships in a custom presentation box. Additional gift wrapping is available for $10.

Q: What if I can't solve my puzzle?
A: Don't give up! But if you're truly stuck, email us and we'll send you a hint (not the full solution -- where's the fun in that?).

Q: Do you offer returns?
A: Due to the handmade nature of our puzzles, all sales are final. However, if your puzzle arrives damaged, we will replace it at no cost.
