Welcome, Puzzle Lovers!

Welcome to Prince of Piece, the Internet's premier destination for handcrafted mechanical puzzles! Every puzzle in our collection is designed and built by hand using the finest woods, metals, and materials. No two puzzles are exactly alike!

Since 1993, our master craftsmen have been creating puzzles that challenge the mind and delight the senses. From intricate puzzle boxes with dozens of hidden moves to elegant trick locks that guard their secrets jealously, each creation is a work of art and engineering.
