~ About Us ~

Our Story

Prince of Piece was founded in 1993 in a small garage workshop in Portland, Oregon. What began as one craftsman's obsession with mechanical puzzles has grown into a respected atelier producing some of the finest handcrafted puzzles in the world.

Our founder discovered his passion for puzzle-making after receiving a Japanese puzzle box as a gift in 1989. Fascinated by the ingenuity of the hidden mechanisms, he began designing his own puzzles -- first as gifts for friends and family, then as a full-time pursuit. The name "Prince of Piece" reflects our belief that every individual piece of a puzzle is royalty -- essential, purposeful, and beautiful in its own right.

Today, our team of four master craftsmen produces over 200 unique puzzles per year, each one designed, built, and finished entirely by hand. Our work has been featured in puzzle competitions worldwide and is held in private collections across six continents.

The Workshop

Our workshop occupies a converted brick warehouse in Portland's industrial district. Inside you'll find a blend of traditional hand tools and precision machinery:

Area What Happens Here
Design Studio Where concepts are born. Hand sketches, technical drawings, and mechanism prototyping on paper before any wood is cut.
Woodshop Traditional woodworking with band saws, scroll saws, lathes, and hand planes. This is where raw timber becomes puzzle components.
Metal Shop Precision metalwork for locks, gears, pins, and mechanisms. Features a small lathe, mill, and brazing station.
Assembly Room Climate-controlled space where puzzles are assembled, tested, adjusted, and tested again until every mechanism is perfect.
Finishing Room Oil finishes, lacquers, and hand-rubbed wax. The final step before a puzzle earns its certificate of authenticity.

Our Philosophy

"A great puzzle is not merely a problem to be solved. It is an experience to be savoured -- a conversation between the maker and the solver, conducted through wood and metal and hidden intention."

-- Our Founder

We believe in:

  • Craftsmanship over production. Every puzzle is made by hand, one at a time. We will never mass-produce.
  • Materials matter. We source the finest hardwoods and metals. A puzzle should feel as good as it looks.
  • Fair challenge. Our puzzles are difficult but never unfair. Every solution is logical and discoverable.
  • Beauty in mechanism. The hidden parts of a puzzle should be as beautiful as the visible ones.
  • Puzzles are for everyone. We make puzzles at every difficulty level, from beginner to expert.
